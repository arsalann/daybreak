# Daybreak privacy policy

Daybreak does not collect, store off-device, or share any personal data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
- No network requests. Your alarms are stored only on your iPhone and scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit.
- Deleting the app deletes its data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/arsalann/daybreak.
